# Shadow of the Colossi

![[undead-giant4_o.jpg]]

Shadows loom over the realm of Ecerah.
An uncertain fate hangs over the people as unbeknownst to do them, heroic deeds of the past are about to be undone.

The Sealing of Vodarr, the god prophet of Ecerah., caused rifts in the within the realm. 
Shifting of power structures, dismantling of the ethos held by religions and countless atrocities committed in the name of change.

You are ordinary people and adventurer, who find themselves in middle of this turmoil, as a greater threat emerges to shake up the foundations of the realm again. Colossal beings taller than the horizon itself, capable of levelling cities in a single step.

With the balance of the world, hanging on by a thread, which threat will you and your friends face first?
